Good work is rarely just the thing that ships. It is the questions asked early, the constraints made visible, and the small decisions that keep a system useful six months later.

This journal is a place for that thinking. We will write about building software with care, making technical choices legible, and finding the practical path through complicated problems.

Thanks for stopping by.
